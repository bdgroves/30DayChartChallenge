## How long we'll live, give or take

Life expectancy at birth in the countries I've visited, from 1950 to the UN's probabilistic projections for 2100, with their 80% and 95% ranges. Found through IHME's Global Health Data Exchange, which catalogs the UN World Population Prospects.

**Data**

- [UN World Population Prospects 2024 (wpp2024 R package)](https://github.com/PPgp/wpp2024)
- [GHDx catalog entry for the UN Population Division](https://ghdx.healthdata.org/organizations/united-nations-population-division)

**Tools:** Python, matplotlib
